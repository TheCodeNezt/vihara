Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Welcome to Vihara ("we", "our", or "us"), a local digital directory and community gallery initiative developed by Team Code Nest. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information shared on our platform.

1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection minimal to ensure a safe, privacy-friendly browsing experience:

  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: When you use features like "Add Place" or "Raise an Issue", you may submit details such as business names, addresses, phone numbers, WhatsApp contact info, and photos.
  • Automatically Collected Technical Data: When visiting Vihara, standard anonymous technical info (such as browser type, device type, and referring URL) may be logged for platform performance and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted to Vihara is used solely for community directory purposes:

  • To list and showcase local shops, stay options, landmarks, and street services in Shravanabelagola.
  • To process requests for updating or removing incorrect business details.
  • To improve the speed, design, and user experience of the platform.

3. Public Display of Business Information

Please note that any business details, phone numbers, or images submitted via the "Add Place" feature are intended for public display in the local directory to help visitors and locals connect with local vendors.

4. Data Accuracy & Requests to Remove / Edit

We strive to keep all directory information accurate. If you are a business owner or resident and notice outdated, missing, or incorrect information associated with your property or shop, you can request an instant review or removal by using the "Raise an Issue" button on the site.

5. Third-Party Services

Vihara is hosted via GitHub Pages and may utilize lightweight third-party services (such as Google Sheets / APIs or web analytics) to fetch directory data smoothly. These providers handle data according to their respective privacy standards.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Vihara does not use tracking cookies for personal profiling. We may use standard browser localStorage purely to save your local UI preferences (such as dark mode settings or filter choices).

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we introduce Phase 2 features. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated timestamp.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this privacy policy or platform details, feel free to reach out to Team Code Nest directly on Instagram or via our platform support channel.
